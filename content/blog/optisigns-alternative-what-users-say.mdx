OptiSigns is one of the most widely used digital signage platforms, and most reviews of it are positive. Ease of use and quick setup come up again and again. But if you are searching for an **OptiSigns alternative**, you are probably reacting to something specific: a price change, a support experience, or a feature you can't get on your plan.

This post summarizes the themes reviewers raise, so you can decide whether switching makes sense. It is not a verdict on OptiSigns. Review sites such as G2 and Capterra were not fully readable for this research, so treat the points below as patterns from individual reviews, not measured statistics.

## What reviewers like about OptiSigns

Reviewers on Trustpilot and G2 praise how quickly a first screen goes live and how many apps and integrations are available. Several single out helpful support agents by name. If those things matter most and your costs are predictable, you may not need to switch at all.

## The themes behind "OptiSigns alternative" searches

**Per-screen pricing that grows.** One Trustpilot reviewer in 2024 described their per-screen cost rising sharply after a new feature tier appeared. Another in 2025 said a paid subscription was needed on top of the player they bought. Both are single reviews, but they point at a common worry: the cost you see at sign-up may not be the cost you pay later.

**Support that varies.** Some 2025 and 2026 reviewers say a support ticket was handed back with a generic reply and nobody took ownership of the issue. Other reviewers in the same period praise the same team. Support quality seems to depend on the agent and the plan.

**Learning curve and complexity.** On G2, "learning curve" and "complexity" are the most common drawbacks listed for OptiSigns, ahead of cost. A platform with hundreds of apps can feel like a lot if you only need menus or announcements on two screens.

**Template limits.** Some reviewers want more flexible templates than they get.

## How to compare any OptiSigns alternative

Before switching, write down the answers for each tool you are considering:

1. **Total monthly cost for your real screen count**, including add-ons, and what happens when you add one more screen.
2. **Cancellation:** how do you cancel, and does anything keep billing after you do?
3. **Who updates the screens.** Test the tool with that person, not the owner.
4. **Hardware:** which players and TVs are supported, and what do they cost?
5. **Support:** channels, hours and response times on the plan you are buying.
6. **Offline behavior:** what plays if the internet drops?
