**Short answer:** your BrightSign players do not stop working in March 2027. What shuts down is the old software around them: **BrightSign Network (BSN.com)** and **BrightAuthor Classic**. If you use either one, you have three options: move to BrightSign's newer BSN.Cloud, keep the players and change the software, or replace the players and the software together.

*Last updated: 2 November 2026. BrightSign may change dates and details, so confirm with their documentation linked below before you act.*

## What is shutting down, and what is not

According to BrightSign's own [legacy documentation](https://docs.brightsign.biz/legacy-docs.md), BrightSign Network (brightsignnetwork.com, often called BSN.com) and BrightAuthor Classic are shutting down in **March 2027**. BrightSign points customers to **BSN.Cloud** and **BrightAuthor:connected** instead.

| | Status |
|---|---|
| BrightSign players (hardware) | Keep working |
| BSN.Cloud and BrightAuthor:connected | The replacement BrightSign recommends |
| BrightSign Network (BSN.com) | Shutting down March 2027 |
| BrightAuthor Classic | Shutting down March 2027 |

Two things to watch for:

- **It is not an account transfer.** BrightSign has a [how-to for migrating local setups](https://docs.brightsign.biz/how-tos/migrate-local-setups-from-brightauthor-classic-to-brightauthorconnectedbsncloud) from BrightAuthor Classic. Presentations and content have to be moved over, and you should plan for some rebuild work.
- **Some older features may not carry over.** A third-party review notes that features such as Bright Wall, Twitter Feed and Mosaic Mode were not yet in the newer software. Check that anything you rely on exists in the new tool before you commit.

## Your three options

| Option | Keep BrightSign players? | Best for | Main trade-off |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Move to BSN.Cloud** | Yes | Larger networks, video walls, teams already trained on BrightSign | You stay in BrightSign's ecosystem and pay its licence costs. Content must be rebuilt in BrightAuthor:connected. |
| **2. Keep the players, change the software** | Yes | Anyone happy with the hardware but not the software | Only works if the new software officially supports BrightSign players. Check before you switch. |
| **3. Replace players and software** | No | Small networks, restaurants, retail shops, anyone wanting lower hardware cost | You buy new players and re-create content, but you leave the ecosystem for good. |

### Option 1: move to BSN.Cloud

This is the path of least change if you are happy with BrightSign. You keep your hardware and your team's skills. The cost is time to rebuild presentations, and continued reliance on one vendor for both hardware and software.

### Option 2: keep the players, change the software

Some third-party signage platforms run on BrightSign hardware. Ask any vendor directly: does it officially support your BrightSign model and series, does it play content offline, and who supports it if something breaks? **DigitalSigns.ai does not have a dedicated BrightSign player app today**, so if you want to stay on BrightSign hardware, confirm support with the vendor in writing before buying.

### Option 3: replace players and software

This is the option that makes the most sense for small networks. A BrightSign player is a premium, purpose-built device. If you run one to ten screens, you may not need that level of hardware.

## Reliability and security: a fair note

You should not leave BrightSign because of a headline. Still, two points are worth checking whichever option you choose:

- **Reliability reports.** Some reviewers describe reboot and firmware problems on newer players, and screens that went black while the dashboard showed a healthy status. These are user reports, not proof of a systemic fault, so test on one screen before you decide.
- **Security patches.** CISA published advisory [ICSA-25-126-03](https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/ics-advisories/icsa-25-126-03), covering CVE-2025-3925 (execution with unnecessary privileges). A related record, CVE-2025-54756, covers a guessable default password on older series 4 and series 5 software. BrightSign released fixes in firmware 8.5.53.1 for series 4 and 9.0.166 for series 5. **If you keep BrightSign players, update the firmware and change default passwords.**

## Migration checklist

1. **Inventory every player.** Record model, series, firmware version, location and what each one shows.
2. **List the features you actually use.** Video walls, live feeds, interactive content, data-driven screens. Anything unusual needs a check against the new platform.
3. **Export presentations and media now.** Do not wait until the final month. BrightSign's legacy tools get harder to rely on as the deadline approaches.
4. **Decide per location.** You can mix: keep BrightSign for a video wall, move simple menu boards to cheaper players.
5. **Test one screen first.** Run the new setup next to the old one for a week.
6. **Run both in parallel.** Switch screens over one at a time, starting with the least critical.
7. **Set a deadline well before March 2027**, so a problem does not land on the last week.

## When staying with BrightSign makes sense

- You run a large network with many BrightSign players already installed and trained staff.
- You need video walls or very demanding multi-zone layouts.
- Your business values a purpose-built, long-lifespan player over lower cost.
- Moving to BSN.Cloud is simple because your content is small.

For these cases, BrightSign's own migration path is probably the right answer.

## Frequently asked questions

**Will my BrightSign players stop working in March 2027?**
No. The shutdown covers BrightSign Network (BSN.com) and BrightAuthor Classic, which are software and services. The players themselves are not what is ending.

**What replaces BSN.com?**
BrightSign recommends BSN.Cloud with BrightAuthor:connected (also called BrightSign Author).

**Can I move my presentations automatically?**
BrightSign provides a how-to for migrating local setups, but content may need to be re-created. Plan time for this and check the official guide.

**Can I use third-party signage software on BrightSign players?**
Some platforms support it. Ask each vendor whether it officially supports your player model and series. DigitalSigns.ai does not have a dedicated BrightSign player app today.

**What is a cheaper alternative to a BrightSign player?**
For small networks, an Amazon Signage Stick, a good Android signage box, or a well-managed Raspberry Pi. See our [player comparison](/blog/best-digital-signage-player/).

**Should I update my BrightSign firmware?**
Yes. CISA's advisory notes fixes in firmware 8.5.53.1 (series 4) and 9.0.166 (series 5). Also change any default passwords.
