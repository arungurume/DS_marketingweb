Yodeck has a loyal following, a free tier and strong overall ratings. Reviewers on Trustpilot often call the dashboard intuitive and set-up quick. So why do people search for a **Yodeck alternative**?

Looking at reviews, the complaints that stand out are mostly about the **player hardware** and about **support and refunds** when something goes wrong. This is a summary of themes from individual reviews, not a statistical ranking, and some review sites could not be read in full.

## What reviewers like about Yodeck

Easy set-up, templates that let you build a display in minutes, and a free plan to try it on one screen. If you are running a small number of screens and the player works, those are real strengths.

## What reviewers complain about

**Players that freeze or drop offline.** A February 2026 Trustpilot reviewer described a device that repeatedly froze, went offline and stopped syncing content. Because Yodeck sells its own player, a faulty device becomes a hardware and a software problem at once.

**Support and refunds.** Several 2025 and 2026 reviewers describe slow or unhelpful support, a return that took weeks, or a refund that was refused. These are individual experiences, but a returns process is something you can check before buying.

**Cost and recurring fees.** Summaries of G2 and Capterra reviews mention per-screen cost as a drawback, along with a learning curve and an interface some find overwhelming.

**Dependence on the internet.** Reviewers note that screens depend on a connection to stay current.

## Questions to ask about any player

- **What happens if the player fails?** Ask for the replacement policy and timeline in writing.
- **Can you use hardware you already own?** A system that runs on common devices avoids being locked to one vendor's player.
- **What plays offline?** Ask whether content is cached on the device and for how long.
- **What does the first month cost, all in?** Software, player, shipping and any add-ons.
- **Can you cancel monthly?**
