A blank or frozen screen during opening hours is the problem reviewers complain about most vividly. Public reviews of several signage platforms describe players that freeze, go offline or stop syncing, sometimes needing someone to visit the site to fix them. If your **digital signage player is offline**, here is what to try first.

These steps are general. Exact menus differ by player and CMS, so check your vendor's help pages for the specifics.

## 7 common fixes

1. **Check the basics.** Confirm the screen has power, the correct HDMI input is selected and the player is plugged in firmly.
2. **Restart the player.** Unplug the player for 30 seconds, then power it on. Many freezes clear with a restart.
3. **Check the network.** Make sure the Wi-Fi or Ethernet connection works by opening a web page on another device on the same network.
4. **Check the device clock.** A wrong date or time on the player can break secure connections to the CMS. Set it to automatic.
5. **Check the firewall.** Corporate or venue networks sometimes block the traffic the player needs. Ask whoever manages the network to allow your CMS.
6. **Update the app.** An out-of-date player app can stop syncing after a platform update. Install the latest version.
7. **Re-pair the screen.** If nothing else works, remove the screen in your CMS dashboard and pair it again with a new code.

## Why screens go offline

The usual causes are an unstable connection, a wrong device clock, a firewall, a frozen low-power device, or an overheated consumer stick running all day. Consumer streaming sticks are designed for a few hours of TV a night, not 24/7 playback.

## What should keep playing offline

Ask any vendor this before you buy: **does the player cache content, and what happens when the connection is lost?** Many players keep playing the last downloaded schedule while offline, but they cannot receive updates until the connection returns. A good setup shows you which screens are offline so you can act.

## Prevent the problem

- Use a purpose-built player where you can, such as the [Amazon Signage Stick](/amazon-signage-stick), made for long-duration playback rather than home streaming.
- Prefer wired Ethernet for screens near the counter.
- Give each location one person who knows how to restart and re-pair a screen.
- Schedule content in advance so a short outage doesn't leave a blank screen.
- Check proof-of-play reports to confirm the screens are actually playing.
