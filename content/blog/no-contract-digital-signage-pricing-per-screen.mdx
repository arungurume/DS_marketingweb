Pricing is one of the most common complaints in digital signage reviews. Not because the software is expensive, but because the total cost is hard to predict. People searching for **no-contract digital signage** or **digital signage pricing per screen** are trying to avoid being surprised later.

This guide covers where surprises come from and how to avoid them. It draws on themes in public reviews, which we could only partially read, so check any vendor's current terms directly.

## Where surprise costs come from

Across review sites, the same kinds of pricing complaints repeat:

- **Price rises after sign-up.** Reviewers describe per-screen costs going up when a plan changes or a new tier launches.
- **Paywalled features.** Something you assumed was included turns out to be paid-only.
- **Hardware plus subscription.** Buying a player and then learning a separate subscription is required.
- **Add-on apps.** Base price covers the editor but not the apps you actually want.
- **Cancellation friction.** One vendor's own help documentation notes you may need to remove licenses as well as cancel the plan, or you can keep being billed.
- **Billing changes without notice.** A reviewer of another platform described screens showing a license-expired message during peak hours after a pricing change they had not been emailed about.

Each is a single report, not a statistic, but together they show what to ask about.

## The all-in cost formula

For any platform, work out:

**(software per screen × screens × months) + (player per screen, one-time) + add-ons + any setup fees**

Do it at your current screen count and at double. Compare the results, not the headline price.

## Questions to ask before you sign

1. Is there a minimum term, and can I cancel monthly?
2. What exactly happens to my screens if a payment fails? Is there a warning?
3. Which features are on which plan? Can you list them?
4. Will the price change when I add screens?
5. What do I need to do to fully stop billing?
6. Will you notify me by email before prices or plans change?
